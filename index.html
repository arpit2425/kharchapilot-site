About KharchaPilot

KharchaPilot is a personal expense-manager app for Android. Its purpose is to help you track your spending effortlessly: it reads your bank and UPI transaction SMS on your device, turns them into categorised expense entries you review and approve, and gives you budgets, reports and insights. KharchaPilot is local-first — your data is stored on your phone. If you choose to enable cloud backup, the app uses Google Drive access (the drive.appdata scope) solely to store your own private backup file in your own Google Drive, so you can restore your data on a new device. The app has no server and the developer never receives or stores your data.

What it does

Everything a personal expense manager needs — with automatic capture as the headline.

📩

Auto-capture from SMS

Detects bank & UPI transaction SMS on-device and extracts amount, merchant, date and type. OTPs and promos are ignored.

✅

Review & categorise

Each captured transaction is a quick Approve / Edit / Ignore. Auto-rules can pre-categorise or auto-approve.

📊

Budgets & reports

Monthly budgets, accounts & transfers, category insights, and CSV / PDF export.

🔒

Private by design

Your data stays in a local database on your phone. No developer server. Optional biometric app lock.

☁️

Your own Google Drive

Opt in to back up to your private Google Drive app folder — auto-backup and restore. We never receive your data.

🇮🇳

Built for India

UPI & major bank SMS formats, ₹ Indian number formatting, and recurring transactions.

Your data stays yours

KharchaPilot is local-first and collects nothing.

  • SMS are parsed entirely on your device
  • Transactions are stored locally on your phone
  • We run no server and collect no personal data
  • Optional backup goes to your own Google Drive
  • SMS content is never uploaded anywhere
  • You can delete your data anytime
