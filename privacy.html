Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Effective: 28 July 2026

KharchaPilot ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal expense-manager Android application published by Arpit Trivedi ("the developer"). This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices. Contact: arpittrivedi2425@gmail.com.

Summary: The app is local-first — your financial data is stored on your device. We operate no server and do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. SMS are processed entirely on your device. Backup is optional — either a file you export yourself, or sync to your own Google Drive under your own Google account — never to us.

1. Information the app accesses

a) SMS messages (optional feature)

If you enable SMS capture and grant the SMS permission, the app reads incoming and existing SMS on your device to identify bank/UPI transaction messages and extract details (amount, merchant/payee, reference, account last-4 digits, debit/credit, date). This processing happens locally using on-device logic. SMS content is never sent to the developer or any third party and is not uploaded anywhere by the app (it is not part of the optional Drive backup). Non-transaction messages (OTPs, personal messages, promotions) are ignored. You can use the app fully without granting this permission by entering transactions manually.

b) Financial/transaction data you create

Transactions, categories, budgets, accounts, and related data are stored in a local database on your device. This is the source of truth.

c) Google account (optional, for backup)

If you enable Google Drive backup, you sign in with Google. The app requests the drive.appdata scope, which lets it read/write only its own private folder in your Google Drive. Your backup file is stored in your Google Drive under your account. The developer has no access to your Google account or Drive contents. Google's handling of your account is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

d) Notifications

The app may show local notifications (e.g., a captured transaction to review). These are generated and shown on-device.

e) Local backup files

You can export your data as a backup file (JSON). The file is created on your device and shared only to the destination you choose (for example your Files app, your own cloud storage, or email); the developer never receives it. Restoring reads a file you select via the Android system file picker — the app accesses only the single file you pick and requires no broad storage permission.

2. How information is used

Solely to provide the app's features on your device: capturing/organising expenses, budgets, reports, and — if you enable it — backing up and restoring your data to your own Google Drive.

3. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone. We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not transmit your data to our servers (we have none).

4. Data storage, retention, and deletion

  • Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.
  • You can delete individual records in-app, and use Data & storage → delete old data to remove older records locally.
  • If you used Drive backup, you can delete the backup from your Google Drive (Drive → Settings → Manage apps → KharchaPilot), and sign out in the app.
  • Any backup files you exported are yours — keep or delete them wherever you saved them.

5. Security

Data is stored within the app's private storage on your device. Backups reside in your private Google Drive app folder. Optionally, enable your device lock / the app's biometric lock for additional protection.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Permissions summary

  • SMS (RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS) — optional; on-device transaction detection.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — show review/alert notifications.
  • Internet / Network state — only to sync with your Google Drive when you enable backup.
  • Biometric — optional app lock.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; changes will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
